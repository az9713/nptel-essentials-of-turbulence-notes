Learning route and prerequisites

Start with multivariable calculus, ordinary and partial differential equations, vector calculus, elementary probability, and undergraduate fluid mechanics. Chapters 6–8 develop the governing equations and statistical tools used later. The numerical examples use Python and NumPy.

  1. Physical foundations, chapters 1–5: recognize turbulent flow, its origins, diffusivity, and characteristic scales.
  2. Equations and statistics, chapters 6–9: connect instantaneous conservation laws to random variables, spectra, Reynolds averaging, and closure.
  3. Cascade and spectral descriptions, chapters 10–15: study Kolmogorov scaling, Fourier modes, spectral tensors, model spectra, and Reynolds-number effects.
  4. Simulation and modeling, chapters 16–20: compare appraisal criteria, DNS, turbulent viscosity, filtering, and LES.

Read the chapters in order, then work through the expandable solutions and reproduce the chapter programs. The source map separates lecture material, research extensions, and original teaching constructions.
